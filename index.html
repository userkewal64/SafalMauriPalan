दशकभन्दा धेरै अनुभवसहित, हामी हाल हरेक वर्ष १०–१५ टन शुद्ध मह उत्पादन गर्दै आएका छौँ। हाम्रो मह विभिन्न प्राकृतिक फूल र बोटबिरुवाबाट संकलित हुने भएकाले प्रत्येक प्रकारको महमा छुट्टै स्वाद, सुगन्ध र गुण पाइन्छ।

The Artisanal Production Process

From flourishing mountain flowers to pure raw honey bottles — how we maintain nature's integrity at every stage.

1

Flora Migration

We position our colonies in clean, unpolluted agricultural zones and wild orchards across Sunsari and eastern Nepal, following the natural flowering cycle of mustard, litchi, and seasonal wildflowers.

2

Natural Capping

We never harvest early. We patiently wait until the bees themselves reduce moisture naturally and seal each honeycomb cell with pure beeswax cappings, confirming optimal maturity and enzyme richness.

3

Cold Extraction

Honeycomb frames are gently uncapped and cold-extracted using centrifugal force in our stainless steel extractor. No boiling, no heating, and no pressure filtering that could destroy natural pollens.

4

Pure Glass Bottling

The freshly harvested honey settles naturally to let micro-bubbles rise. It is then hand-filled into clean, food-grade glass jars, preserving aroma, active compounds, and taste.
